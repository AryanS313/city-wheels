Elevation: USGS National Map 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), public-domain bare-earth DEM. Source service: https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer

The tile is 1,000 × 1,000 metres in EPSG:32610, centered on 37.8024° N, 122.4169° W. Terrain is sampled at 3.90625 m spacing from the real USGS multi-resolution mosaic. OSM-tagged building heights are preserved; missing heights and facade styles are explicitly marked estimates in `city.json`.

Original OSM extracts, elevation TIFF, service/catalog metadata, request URLs, timestamps, and checksums are distributed with the source repository under `data/cities/san-francisco/sources`. The game code has its own separate license.
