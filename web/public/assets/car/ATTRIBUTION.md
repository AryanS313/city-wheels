City Wheels adaptations: runtime coordinate/unit normalization, separate animated wheels, material recoloring, merged draw groups, lightweight traffic interiors, local impact dents, projected scratches and windshield crack overlay. The original source GLB is preserved. The license-plate and steering-emblem objects are omitted from the rendered adaptation.
