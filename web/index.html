A first playable district, with real OSM roads and USGS elevation. A detailed concept car, physical traffic, pedestrians and solid roadside objects bring the streets to life. Buildings remain procedural. This browser edition does not use Unreal, Lumen or Nanite.

Injuring a pedestrian, a fatal collision or stealing a car starts a police response. Hide from police for 60 continuous seconds to escape. If officers hold you at a stop, you are busted. Collision outcomes are fictional and non-graphic.

The native UE5 source and map import tools are included in the project. A compiled Unreal release is pending an Unreal build machine.
